Spot the pattern

Scams come in different forms.

Knowing the common tactics can make suspicious messages easier to recognize.

Phishing

Fake emails and websites imitate trusted organizations to steal passwords, payment details, or personal information.

QRishing

A malicious QR code can send you to a fake sign-in or payment page designed to capture your information.

Smishing

Text messages use urgent warnings or rewards to get you to tap a link or share a verification code.

Vishing

A caller impersonates a bank or agency and pressures you to reveal a one-time password or send money.

Fake online shops

Fraudulent listings lure shoppers with unusually low prices, then take payment without delivering the item.

Impersonation

Someone pretends to be a friend, delivery service, or public official to ask for money or private details.

Community review

Some reports need a human verdict.

When AI cannot confidently tell whether a report is a scam, volunteer reviewers examine its evidence. Once reviewers agree, the verdict is published for that report.
